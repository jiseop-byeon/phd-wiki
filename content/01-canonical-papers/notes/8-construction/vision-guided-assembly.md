**Feng, Xiao, Willette, McGee & Kamat · Automation in Construction 2015** — [paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autcon.2015.06.002)

## English

**One-line summary:** A mobile construction robot localizes at an unstructured work face,
uses vision to assemble components, and scans the result back into an as-built model.

The enduring idea is the closed geometry loop: design → mobile localization → vision-
guided manipulation → as-built scanning. It anchors the Michigan construction-
manipulation lineage and exposes the “reversed spatial relationship”: the manipulator
moves to the structure rather than a fixtured product arriving at a robot.

> [!warning] Reading the claim
> Marker-based metrology and prepared components reduce site uncertainty. Treat this as a
> foundational integrated workflow, not evidence that arbitrary unstructured assembly was
> solved.

## 한국어

**한 줄:** 모바일 건설 로봇이 비정형 작업면에 위치를 맞추고 비전으로 부품을 조립한 뒤 결과를
as-built 모델로 다시 스캔한다. 설계 → 이동 정합 → 조작 → 검증의 폐루프가 미시간 조작 계보의
출발점이다. 마커와 준비된 부품의 가정을 임의 현장 일반성으로 확대하면 안 된다.
