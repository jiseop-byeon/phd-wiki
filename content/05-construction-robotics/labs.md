## English

Who does construction robotics research, and what each group is known for. Links verified;
US-centric per the field's academic weight, with the ETH cluster as the global reference
point. (For companies see [[03-deep-learning/physical-ai-ecosystem|the ecosystem page]].)

### United States

| Lab | PI | Known for |
|---|---|---|
| [RICAL](https://rical.ce.gatech.edu/index.html), Georgia Tech CEE | Yong K. Cho | field-task robotization: construction ops, infrastructure maintenance, worker safety; LiDAR/scan automation |
| [RAAMAC](https://raamac.cee.illinois.edu), UIUC CEE | Mani Golparvar-Fard | computer-vision progress monitoring from site photos/BIM; spun out **Reconstruct Inc.** |
| [LIVE Lab](https://live.engin.umich.edu) & CEE robotics group, Univ. of Michigan | Vineet Kamat, Carol Menassa | human-robot collaboration on site, worker-assist robots, interactive visualization; one of the largest US construction-automation programs |
| [DPM Lab](https://dpm.engin.umich.edu), Univ. of Michigan | SangHyun Lee | worker-centered construction automation: wearable biosensing, safety/productivity analytics, robots-for-humans; a large academic family tree (see below) |
| RAISE Lab ([profile](https://cee.illinois.edu/directory/profile/hjebelli)), UIUC CEE | Houtan Jebelli | construction robotics + physiological computing, human-robot interaction on site; DPM (Lee) PhD alumnus |
| [CIFE](https://cife.stanford.edu), Stanford | (center, multi-PI) | the long-running home of VDC/BIM research — the digital-model side that site robots consume |
| USC (legacy) | Behrokh Khoshnevis | **Contour Crafting** — the origin of construction 3D printing |
| UT Austin (adjacent groups) | Luis Sentis (HCRL), Mitch Pryor (NRG) | humanoid/whole-body control and field robotics — the robotics-side neighbors of a construction program |

Plus Meiyin Liu (Rutgers), JoonOh Seo (HK PolyU), Byungjoo Choi (Ajou) — one of the
densest advisor networks in construction automation research. Watching this tree's output
≈ watching where US construction robotics is heading.

### How to use this map

- **Paper triage**: an unfamiliar construction-robotics paper is usually traceable to one
  of these clusters — knowing the cluster tells you the assumptions (CV-monitoring vs
  fabrication vs heavy-machine autonomy vs HRC).
- **Venue watch**: these groups publish in Automation in Construction, J. Computing in
  Civil Engineering, ISARC, and increasingly ICRA/IROS — the same split as
  [[05-construction-robotics/index|the venue list]].
- **For this wiki's direction** (learning-based/physical AI in construction): ETH RSL and
  Michigan are the closest methodological neighbors; RAAMAC/RICAL define the perception
  baseline; the [[05-construction-robotics/lineage|lineage page]] shows where the field's
  open question sits.

### 이 지도를 쓰는 법

- **논문 분류**: 낯선 건설로봇 논문은 대개 이 클러스터 중 하나로 거슬러 올라간다 —
  클러스터를 알면 그 논문의 가정(CV 모니터링 vs 패브리케이션 vs 중장비 자율성 vs 협업)이
  보인다.
- **학회 감시**: 이 그룹들은 Automation in Construction, J. Computing in Civil
  Engineering, ISARC, 그리고 점점 ICRA/IROS에 낸다 —
  [[05-construction-robotics/index|학회 목록]]과 같은 분할이다.
- **이 위키의 방향에서** (건설의 학습 기반/physical AI): 방법론적으로 가장 가까운 이웃은
  ETH RSL과 미시간이고, RAAMAC/RICAL이 인식의 베이스라인을 정의하며, 분야의 열린 질문이
  어디에 있는지는 [[05-construction-robotics/lineage|계보 페이지]]가 보여준다.
